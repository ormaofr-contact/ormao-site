Le prompt

A traditional Japanese ukiyo-e woodblock print of a spring kite festival on a breezy grassy hill overlooking a river valley. Many geometric kites fly in a pale sky fading from cream to light blue, adorned with carp, crane, and wave patterns in indigo, vermilion, and ochre. Foreground: villagers in patterned kimono and straw hats pulling taut strings, a child running with a red kite. Wind-swept pine trees leaning, stylized gust lines. Curved wooden bridge over the river, terraced rice paddies and distant temple roof near a flat-topped mountain. Crisp black key-block outlines, subtle bokashi gradients, visible washi paper texture, wood grain marks. Edo period aesthetic, joyful mood, no modern elements, no text, vertical 3:4 ratio.
