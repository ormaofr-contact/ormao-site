Comment l’utiliser

  1. Copie le prompt ou ouvre-le dans ton IA.

Le prompt

A charming 3D isometric diorama of a small two-story corner French bakery at dawn, floating on a square cobblestone slab, soft cream background. Cutaway dollhouse view. Ground floor: glowing brick bread oven, baker in white apron sliding baguettes on a wooden peel, cooling racks of croissants, marble counter with brass scale and macaron display. Upstairs: tiny cozy flat, quilted bed, sleeping orange cat on windowsill, steaming teapot. Outside: mint-and-white striped awning, croissant-shaped wooden sign without text, bicycle with bread basket, lit lamppost. Golden morning light, soft shadows, pastel palette of butter yellow, terracotta and mint. Clay-like materials, tilt-shift miniature feel, highly detailed, clean render, 1:1 square ratio.
